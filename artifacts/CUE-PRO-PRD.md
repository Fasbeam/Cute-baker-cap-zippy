# Product Requirements Document (PRD): "CUE-PRO" — Indian Markets Confluence & Risk-Managed Trading Engine

**Project:** Cute Trader Pro / CUE-PRO  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-11-11  

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy
CUE-PRO is a non-executing, rule-based decision-support and signal intelligence application for Indian Equities (Nifty 200 Cash & F&O) and Index Derivatives (Nifty 50, Bank Nifty).

**Core Philosophy:** "Silence is a recommendation." The app generates a trade card only when Institutional Order Flow (OI/Delivery), Price Action (RDX + Moving Averages), Volatility Regime (India VIX/IVP), and Short-Term Macro/Event Filters all align.

**Mandatory Hedging:** Every derivative trade idea generated by the engine must be strictly defined-risk (Credit Spreads, Debit Spreads, Iron Condors, or Protective-Put Hedged Futures). Naked futures and naked OTM option buying are blocked at the architecture level.

## 2. System Architecture & Data Ingestion Pipelines
### A. Market Data Feeds (Real-Time & EOD)
- Indices & Equities: Real-time OHLCV (1-min, 5-min, 15-min, 1-hour, 4-hour, Daily) for Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, India VIX, Sectoral Indices (Nifty Bank, IT, Auto, Pharma, Metal, PSU Bank, FMCG), and all Nifty 200 constituents.
- Derivatives Chain (NSE): Live Option Chain across 3 expiries (Current Weekly, Next Weekly, Monthly) capturing Strike Price, LTP, Bid/Ask, IV, Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Total OI, and Intraday Change in OI (COI).
- Cash Market Institutional Data: Daily NSE Bhavcopy / Delivery Percentage and Bulk/Block deal data for Nifty 200 stocks.

### B. Event & Macro Calendar Ingestion (Automated Risk Shield)
The app must ingest an economic and corporate event calendar via financial news/calendar APIs and apply automated Event-Shield Rules:
- **Macro Events:** RBI MPC Interest Rate Decisions, US Fed FOMC Rate Decisions, India/US CPI Inflation prints, Union Budget, and Crude Oil shock alerts.
- **Corporate Events (Stock-Specific):** Quarterly Earnings (Results) dates, Dividend/Bonus/Split ex-dates, and SEBI/Exchange ASM/GSM surveillance additions.

## 3. The 5-Pillar Quantitative Decision Engine
A trade card is generated only when all 5 pillars pass sequentially. If any pillar fails or conflicts, the system outputs a "NO TRADE / SILENCE" status with the exact reason.

### Pillar 1: Time, Spike & Event-Shield Gate (Pre-Filter)
- **Opening Stabilization Lock:** Suppress all signal generation between 09:15 AM and 10:15 AM IST to avoid opening VIX spikes and false gap-up/gap-down traps.
- **Chasing Prevention Filter:** Block long entries if Spot Price is within 0.15% of the Intraday High, and block short entries if Spot is within 0.15% of the Intraday Low. Signals trigger only on pullbacks to VWAP, 5-EMA, or 38.2%–50% Fibonacci retracements.
- **Macro & Earnings Shield:**
  - Stock Earnings Lock: Automatically suppress new F&O or Cash swing signals on any stock within 3 trading days prior to its quarterly earnings announcement (unless explicitly toggled to an "Earnings Volatility Strategy").
  - Central Bank Policy Lock (RBI / Fed): Within 24 hours prior to an RBI MPC or US FOMC rate decision, block all naked directional spreads and restrict index setups strictly to Iron Condors or No Trade until 30 minutes post-announcement.

### Pillar 2: Volatility Regime Engine (India VIX & IV Percentile)
The app calculates India VIX trend and 252-day IV Percentile (IVP) for the underlying asset to select the mathematically optimal strategy structure:
- **High Volatility / Fear Regime (India VIX > 15 OR IVP > 45):** Preferred Structure: Net Credit Spreads (Bull Put Spread, Bear Call Spread, Iron Condor) to harvest inflated option premiums and benefit from volatility crush.
- **Low / Normal Volatility Regime (India VIX <= 15 AND IVP <= 45):** Preferred Structure: Net Debit Spreads (Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread) or Hedged Futures (1 Lot Future + 1 Lot ATM Put) to avoid low-yield credit risk and sudden VIX spike drawdowns.

### Pillar 3: Institutional Order Flow (50% Decision Weight)
**For Indices (Nifty / Bank Nifty):**
- Aggregate Intraday Change in OI (COI) across Current + Next + Far expiries.
- Bullish Wall: Net Put COI added below spot significantly exceeds Call COI (Put COI / Call COI > 1.3).
- Bearish Wall: Net Call COI added above spot significantly exceeds Put COI (Call COI / Put COI > 1.3).
- Unwinding Filter: If both Put COI and Call COI are negative (short covering / long unwinding), classify as Unwinding (No Fresh Wall) and suppress credit spread signals.

**For Nifty 200 Stocks:**
- Bullish Institutional Footprint: Futures Price ↑ + Futures OI ↑ (Long Buildup) + Cash Delivery % > 45% on breakout candle + fresh ATM/OTM-1 Put writing.
- Bearish Institutional Footprint: Futures Price ↓ + Futures OI ↑ (Short Buildup) + fresh ATM/OTM-1 Call writing.

### Pillar 4: RDX Price Action & Trend Architecture (30% Decision Weight)
**Secular & Sector Filter (Stocks Only):**
- Long swings permitted only if Spot > 200-day SMA AND the stock's parent Sectoral Index is outperforming Nifty 50 over the past 5 sessions.

**RDX Momentum Matrix (RSI 14 + ADX 14):**
- Quiet / Range-Bound Tape: RSI between 40 and 60 AND ADX < 20 (flat/falling).
- Confirmed Uptrend: RSI between 55 and 72, ADX > 20 and rising, Spot > VWAP, Spot > 20-EMA, and 5-EMA sloping upward above 20-EMA.
- Confirmed Downtrend: RSI between 28 and 45, ADX > 20 and rising, Spot < VWAP, Spot < 20-EMA, and 5-EMA sloping downward below 20-EMA.

### Pillar 5: Multi-Timeframe Swing Confluence (20% Decision Weight — Swings Only)
- Check the 4-Hour Impulse Swing Fibonacci Retracement.
- Swing setups trigger only when the 5-EMA and 20-EMA slopes agree on both Daily and 4-Hour charts, and price pulls back into the 38.2% – 50% Fibonacci support/resistance zone without breaking the 61.8% structural invalidation level.
- **Expiry Rule:** Intraday setups may use Current Weekly expiry. Swing setups must use Next Weekly or Monthly expiry.

## 4. Strategy Selection Matrix (Automated Card Generator)
| Market State (Pillar 2 + 3 + 4) | Generated Trade Card | Strike Selection Logic |
|---------------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Quiet Tape + High IVP + Put Wall | Bull Put Credit Spread | Sell Strike at highest Put COI wall; Buy Hedge 200 pts (Nifty) / 2–3 strikes (Stock) lower. |
| Quiet Tape + High IVP + Call Wall | Bear Call Credit Spread | Sell Strike at highest Call COI wall; Buy Hedge 200 pts (Nifty) / 2–3 strikes (Stock) higher. |
| Quiet Tape + High IVP + Balanced Walls | Iron Condor | Sell Put Wall + Call Wall; Buy outer wings on both sides for hard loss cap. |
| Strong Uptrend + Low/Mid IVP + Bullish OI | Bull Call Debit Spread | Buy ATM Call; Sell 1-step OTM Call at immediate resistance. |
| Strong Downtrend + Low/Mid IVP + Bearish OI | Bear Put Debit Spread | Buy ATM Put; Sell 1-step OTM Put at immediate support. |
| Strong Stock Breakout + 200-SMA Clear | Hedged Future OR Cash Swing | Buy 1 Lot Future + Buy 1 Lot ATM Put (or Cash Equity with 1% account risk). |

## 5. User Interface (UI) & Trade Card Specification
### Dashboard Layout
- **Top Market Pulse Bar:** Live Ticker for Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, India VIX (with Regime Badge: Credit-Friendly vs. Debit-Friendly), Sector Heatmap (Top 2 & Bottom 2 Sectors), and Active Macro Event Countdown Banner.
- **Main Feed ("Cue Cards"):** Displays only active, high-confluence cards. When no setup qualifies, displays a calm "Market Silence — Capital Protected" card explaining which pillars disagree.
- **Interactive Position Sizing Calculator:** User inputs Total Capital and Max Risk % per trade (default 1.5%); the app auto-calculates exact lot count / share quantity.

### Anatomy of a Trade Card
Every generated card must display:
- **Header:** Underlying Symbol, Trade Type (Intraday / Swing), Strategy Name (e.g., Nifty Bull Put Spread — Nov 26 Expiry), and Confluence Score (85/100).
- **The "Why" (3-Line Human-Readable Proof):**
  - OI Proof: e.g., "+42L Put COI added at 25,100 vs +11L Call COI across 3 expiries."
  - RDX & VIX Proof: e.g., "RSI at 61.4, ADX rising at 24.2, India VIX cooling at 16.2."
  - Macro/Event Check: e.g., "Clear — No RBI/Fed events or earnings for 12 days."
- **Exact Execution Legs:** Buy Leg first (for margin benefit), Sell Leg second, Net Credit/Debit, Max Profit, Hard-Capped Max Loss, and Breakeven Price.
- **Invalidation Wall (Stop-Loss):** Exact Spot closing level that invalidates the thesis + Spread Stop-Loss level (50% of entry debit or 1:1 risk-to-reward on credit).

## 6. Post-Entry Risk Management & Anti-Tilt Guardrails
The app includes an active Trade Journal & Circuit-Breaker Module to enforce institutional discipline:
- **50% Profit Scale-Out Alert:** Sends a push notification when a spread reaches 50% of max profit (or 1:1.5 R:R in cash), instructing the user to book 50% quantity and trail the remaining stop-loss to breakeven.
- **OI Flip Kill-Alert:** Monitors live COI after entry; if the intraday Change-in-OI flips against the trade direction, triggers an immediate "Thesis Broken — Exit Now" alert.
- **Expiry-Day Gamma Guard:** Automatically alerts the user to close all weekly credit spreads by 1:30 PM IST on expiry day.
- **Revenge-Trading Lockout (30-Day Ticker Ban):** Tracks user-logged trades. If the user logs 2 consecutive losses on the same stock ticker (e.g., Wipro), the app locks and hides all future trade cards for that ticker for 30 calendar days.
- **Weekly Churn & Loss Cap:** Tracks weekly trade count (warning at >3 trades/week) and weekly net drawdown (triggering a "Kill Switch Recommended" lock screen if weekly losses hit 3% of capital).

## 7. Suggested Tech Stack for AI App Builders
- **Frontend:** React / Next.js + Tailwind CSS + TradingView Lightweight Charts (for plotting Spot, VWAP, 5/20 EMA, 200-SMA, and OI Walls).
- **Backend & Quant Engine:** Python (FastAPI) + Pandas / NumPy / TA-Lib (for real-time RSI, ADX, Fibonacci, and COI calculations).
- **Data & Broker APIs:** Kite Connect API / Upstox API / DhanHQ API (for live option chains, India VIX, and basket-order deep links) + Financial Modeling Prep / TradingEconomics API (for macro & earnings calendars).
